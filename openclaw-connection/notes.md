I connected OpenClaw to Telegram by creating a Telegram bot to interact with it.
I also used Zapier MCP to connect OpenClaw to Google Drive and Google Calendar.
For security reasons, I created a separate Google account specifically for this task.
The setup required configuring the different connections and making sure the permissions were correct.
The most challenging part was getting all the services connected and properly authenticated.
After completing the setup, I ran several tests to check if everything worked as expected.
The tests were successful, and OpenClaw was able to create documents in Google Drive and schedule events in Google Calendar.
Overall, the integration worked well and demonstrated how OpenClaw can automate tasks through Telegram.
